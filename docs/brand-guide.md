# Tanzstudio Bonita — Brand Guide

## Brand identity

**Brand name:** Tanzstudio Bonita

**Tagline:** Dein Ort zum Tanzen, Lachen und Wohlfühlen.

**Brand direction:** Warm boutique studio

**Brand personality:**
- Warm and welcoming
- Joyful and energetic
- Feminine, elegant and modern
- Inclusive and community-focused
- Approachable rather than intimidating

## Colour palette

Our palette combines warm neutrals with soft, earthy accents.

| Colour | Hex | Intended use |
|---|---|---|
| Warm ivory | `#F4EFE7` | Main background |
| Taupe | `#B5A397` | Secondary text and neutral accents |
| Terracotta | `#B96F59` | Primary brand accent |
| Dusty rose | `#D8B5B2` | Soft highlights and secondary accents |
| Soft sage | `#A2A78F` | Supporting accent |

### Colour usage

- **Warm ivory** should be the main background colour.
- **Terracotta** is the primary accent and should provide recognisable brand character.
- **Taupe** is used for understated typography and supporting elements.
- **Dusty rose** adds softness and warmth.
- **Soft sage** can be used sparingly for variety in the wider visual identity.

Avoid using all five colours equally. Keep the overall appearance calm, warm and balanced.

## Logo direction

The primary logo combines:
- Elegant script lettering for "Bonita"
- Clean, understated typography for "Tanzstudio"
- A joyful female dancer with long, wavy, flowing hair
- Long trousers and sneakers
- A dynamic Zumba-inspired pose
- A warm, earthy colour treatment

The dancer should feel energetic and expressive, not like a ballet dancer.

The logo should remain elegant, recognisable and readable at smaller sizes.

## Typography

- **Bonita:** Elegant flowing script lettering.
- **Tanzstudio:** Clean, modern sans-serif lettering, preferably uppercase or understated title case.

Maintain a clear contrast between the expressive wordmark and the simple supporting typography.

## Visual principles

1. Prefer warm, natural and soft colours.
2. Keep layouts clean and uncluttered.
3. Use movement-inspired curves and shapes sparingly.
4. Prioritise readability and consistency.
5. Make the studio feel welcoming to both children and adults.
